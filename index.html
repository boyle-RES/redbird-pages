Rhythm icon maker 2.0

Shorts per sound: 0.5 = sixteenth, 1 = short, 1.5 = dotted eighth, 2 = long, 3 = dotted quarter, 4 = lo-ong, 8 = whole. Type R and a length for a rest, such as R1. Put ~ between two sounds to join them, such as 8~ 8: a tie when the pitch stays the same, a slur when it changes. Type // where a new line of icons should start. Bar lines (|) are skipped.

One per sound: letter names (C, F#, Bb) or numbers in the key (1 = the key’s first note). A letter goes to the octave nearest the note before it; add an octave number for a bigger leap (G3). Numbers stay in the key’s home octave; add + or - for the octave above or below (1+, 5-), and # or b to raise or lower one (#4, b7). A dash (-) repeats the pitch. Leave this empty for a rhythm on one level.

One syllable per sound, such as Twin- kle. Or type whole words and choose Split into syllables, then fix any breaks by hand. Type _ to hold a syllable over the next sound, or a dash (-) to leave a sound without one.

A time signature such as 2/4 or 6/8 adds beat bars under the icons and sets what Tempo counts. Leave it blank for none. Pickup is how many shorts come before the first downbeat, such as 1 for an eighth-note pickup or 2 for a quarter-note pickup.

The key for numbers in Pitches. 1 is the key’s first note, between middle C and the B above it, and the other numbers count up the scale from there (8 is the 1 above). Letter names on the notes follow the key, so 4 in F major shows as B♭. Letter names you type aren’t changed by the key.

Pitch range

With “Same for the whole song,” every pattern is drawn on the same pitch range, so frames and icon heights match from pattern to pattern. Load each of the song’s patterns and choose Include this pattern; the range grows to cover them all.
