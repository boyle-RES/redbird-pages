    Music

    Lengths in shorts, separated by spaces: 0.5 sixteenth, 1 short, 1.5 dotted short, 2 long, 3 dotted long, 4 lo-ong, 6 dotted lo-ong, 8 whole. Put R in front for a rest (R2). Add ~ to let a sound ring (8~). A | between measures is optional.

    shortlongdotted longlo-ongwhole

    Letter names, one per sound (G, F#, Bb). Add an octave number to pick the octave (G3, C5); otherwise each note goes in the octave closest to the one before. Join notes with + for a chord (G4+B4+D5). Leave blank for unpitched instruments. If there are fewer pitches than sounds, the last one repeats.
